One-page data and methodology note

How this decision tool was built

Decision question. Where should the Cambridge Community Development Department concentrate limited storefront outreach and small-business support? The primary user is a city economic-development analyst preparing a district-level action plan.

Data collection

Four public sources were downloaded on 2026-09-21 by reproducible Python scripts: Cambridge’s vacant storefront snapshots and cottage-food permits, Boston’s food establishment licenses, Census County Business Patterns 2022, and BLS Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages 2024 Q1. Socrata row counts were checked before download to prevent silent truncation.

Privacy and cleaning

Phone numbers were deleted from the Boston file before it was written. The cottage-food source describes home businesses, so names, business names, phone numbers, email addresses, street addresses, and coordinates were never persisted. Cambridge storefront owner and leasing-contact fields were also removed. District spelling variants were merged into stable display categories; floor-area ranges were converted to their midpoint.

Measures

Vacant storefronts
Count of rows in the latest available Cambridge snapshot: September 2025.
Estimated vacancy months
Months between the snapshot and the reported vacancy year. Because only a year is provided, July is used as a midpoint assumption and negative values are capped at zero.
Long-term vacancy
A record with an estimated vacancy duration greater than 12 months.
Priority score
District storefront count multiplied by estimated average vacancy months. It ranks outreach urgency; it is not a probability of success.
Area bands
Under 1,000; 1,000–2,500; over 2,500 square feet; or not reported.
Industry context
County-level establishments, employment, and annual payroll for retail, food services, and personal services.

Geographic comparability

Cambridge business districts come directly from the storefront file after label normalization. Boston ZIP codes and coordinates describe food-license records only. Middlesex and Suffolk County statistics provide broad regional context. These three levels are not interchangeable, so the site does not calculate city-to-city vacancy or market-share comparisons.

What this supports

The evidence supports comparing current reported vacancies across Cambridge districts, identifying space and duration patterns, and prioritizing where staff should investigate first. It does not establish why a storefront is vacant, estimate customer demand, measure rent affordability, or prove that a proposed business will be profitable.

Reproduce

Run the four fetch/build scripts in pipeline/, then run python3 pipeline/verify_pipeline.py. The website reads only generated JSON and privacy-safe downloadable CSV files. Full commands are in the project README.
